2The FlatSat

A FlatSat is every piece of hardware on our satellite, mounted flat on a rack instead of stacked inside the 2U structure. With everything spread out, every board can be probed, rewired, and swapped while we integrate and test.

I'm in charge of ours. When a subteam hands me hardware, I integrate it onto the rack and connect it to the rest of the system, especially the power wiring. Then I watch the current draws to make sure the power system can supply every subteam at once.

What I check on every integration
  • Power wiring matches the interface documents
  • Each subsystem's current draw under power
  • Total draw stays inside the power budget
  • Hardware-software interfaces behave as specified

5What I do

FlatSat & integration

In charge of the FlatSat. I integrate the hardware each subteam gives me, handle the power wiring between boards, watch current draws, and run the system integration test plans that check hardware-software interfaces against program-level dispenser and safety requirements.

Power

Work with the power subteam in the lab on an in-house, aerospace-grade battery, making sure it meets its requirements, and work with every other subteam to make sure each one gets the power it needs.

Architecture & documentation

Develop and maintain CONOPS diagrams, Interface Control Documents, and software topology requirements so every subteam builds to the same interfaces.

Mission planning & risk

Model orbital data and simulate mission stages in Systems Tool Kit (STK) to validate orbits and communication windows. Track mass, power, and link budgets, and mission risks through FMEA.
